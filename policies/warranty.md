# Warranty

## Coverage
All Halden Outfitters own-brand gear has a 2 year warranty against defects in materials and workmanship. Other brands carry their maker's warranty, usually 1 year.

## What is not covered
Normal wear, misuse, and damage from accidents are not covered.

## Claims
Warranty claims after the 90 day damaged-item window are handled by the repairs team, not by refund. Escalate the conversation with the order number and a description of the fault.
