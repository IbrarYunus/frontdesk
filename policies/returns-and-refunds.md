# Returns and refunds

## Change of mind
Customers can return any full-price item within 30 days of delivery for a full refund of the item price. The item does not need a reason. Shipping charges are not refunded.

## Final sale
Items marked final sale (clearance) cannot be returned or refunded for change of mind. They are still covered if they arrive damaged or the wrong item was sent.

## Damaged or wrong item
If an item arrives damaged, or is not the item ordered, the customer can get a full refund of that item within 90 days of delivery. This includes final sale items. We do not ask for the item back if it is damaged.

## How refunds are paid
Refunds go back to the original payment method and take 5 to 7 business days to appear. Refunds above £100 are reviewed by a supervisor before they are paid, usually within one business day.

## One refund per item
An item can be refunded once. If an item was already refunded, it cannot be refunded again.
