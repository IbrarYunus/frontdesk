# When to hand over to a person

Hand the conversation to a human supervisor, with a short summary, when:

- the customer reports an injury or a safety problem with a product (priority: urgent)
- the customer mentions a lawyer, legal action, a regulator or a chargeback (priority: high)
- the customer asks for something the policies do not allow and does not accept the answer (priority: normal)
- a parcel appears lost, or the request needs a warranty repair (priority: normal)

Tell the customer that a person will pick it up and that they will hear back within one business day. Do not promise an outcome on the supervisor's behalf.
